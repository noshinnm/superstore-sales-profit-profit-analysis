# Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis

##  Project Overview

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** built using the Superstore dataset to analyze sales performance, profitability, customers, products, categories, segments, and regional performance.

The project focuses on turning raw sales data into actionable business insights through interactive visualizations and DAX-based KPIs.

## Tools & Skills

* Power BI
* DAX
* Data Cleaning & Transformation
* Data Visualization
* Business Analysis
* KPI Analysis
* Profitability Analysis

##  Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Sales & Profit Dashboard

Provides an overview of overall business performance, including:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Orders
* Profit Margin
* Average Order Value
* Sales & Profit by Category
* Sales & Profit by Region
* Sales by Segment
* Sales & Profit by Sub-Category
* Interactive filters for Date, Region, Category and Segment


### 2. Customer & Product Analysis

Provides deeper analysis of customer and product performance, including:

* Top 10 Customers by Sales
* Top 10 Customers by Profit
* Top 10 Products by Sales
* Top 10 Products by Profit
* Loss-Making Products
* Profit Margin by Sub-Category
* Key Business Insights


##  Key Business Insights

* **Paper** generates a strong **43.39% profit margin** despite relatively low sales.
* **Tables** have the weakest profit margin at **−8.56%**, followed by Bookcases and Supplies.
* **Copiers, Phones and Accessories** are among the strongest sub-categories by total profit.
* **Sean Miller** generates high sales but does not rank among the top customers by profit, showing that high revenue does not necessarily mean high profitability.
* Several products generate negative profit and may require further review of pricing, discounting and cost efficiency.

##  Business Recommendations

* Review pricing and cost structures for consistently loss-making products.
* Evaluate discounting strategies for low-margin products.
* Prioritize high-margin products and sub-categories where appropriate.
* Evaluate customer performance using both revenue and profitability rather than sales alone.
* Compare regional performance using both sales and profit metrics.

##  Learning Outcome

This project helped develop practical skills in **Power BI, DAX, data visualization, profitability analysis, and business storytelling** by transforming a raw sales dataset into an interactive business intelligence dashboard.
